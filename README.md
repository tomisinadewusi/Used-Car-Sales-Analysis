#  Used Car Sales Analysis

### Discount Motors | Exploratory Data Analysis

An exploratory analysis of **6,738 used-car records** to evaluate fuel-type sales, pricing patterns, and the potential market opportunity for hybrid vehicles.

##  Business Objective

Discount Motors wanted to understand how **hybrid sales compare with other fuel types** and generate insights to support its marketing strategy around the transition toward lower-emission vehicles.

##  Approach

- **Data validation:** verified data types, categorical values, year ranges, and dataset structure.
- **Data cleaning:** checked for missing values and removed **39 duplicate records**, resulting in **6,699 unique records**.
- **EDA:** analysed price distributions, fuel-type composition, and descriptive statistics.
- **Business analysis:** compared sales volumes and average prices across fuel types.
- **Visualisation:** communicated findings using Python-based charts.

##  Key Findings

- **Petrol accounted for 61%** of sales, making it the dominant fuel type.
- **Hybrid vehicles accounted for 30%** of sales, indicating substantial representation within the dataset.
- Hybrid vehicles had an average price of **£17,188**, compared with **£9,755** for petrol vehicles.
- Diesel represented **7%** of sales, while other fuel types accounted for **2%**.
- No electric vehicles were present in the dataset, aligning with the business context that none had been sold during the period considered.

## Tools

**Python · Pandas · Matplotlib · Jupyter/Google Colab**

##  Business Insight

The analysis highlights a meaningful hybrid presence alongside continued petrol dominance. The significant price difference between hybrid and petrol vehicles is an important consideration for **pricing, customer segmentation, and future inventory strategy**.
